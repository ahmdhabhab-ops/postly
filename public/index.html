    New customers

    Your AI team searches the public web for people and businesses likely to buy from you. You contact them yourself.

    1. Your ideal customer

    Not built yet. It uses what you wrote in Competitors > About your business.

    2. Find new customers

    The AI looks for businesses that need what you sell, and for public posts where someone says they are looking for it. It can take a minute. One search per day.

    How this works. Only public pages are used. We never collect emails, phone numbers or names of private people, and nothing is sent for you: you write to each lead yourself. Please do not message people who have not asked to hear from you: WhatsApp and email rules require consent. "High intent" means the page shows a clear need, a date or a budget. It is never a guarantee, so check each lead before you spend time.
